Tone alone cannot tell a table drawn in shadow from the empty
    page around the map. Cutting the background out settles it, and the map then
    reads by its transparency rather than by how dark things are. The first cut
    downloads a model, once.

3 Read the map

  

    
    
  

  
Instant and free. Floors keep their cells; walls are drawn as
    lines on the boundaries between cells, because that is what they are.

  

    
  






  
4 Palette

  

    
    
  

  

    Which asset packs to build from
    

    
Leave the sci-fi pack out of a dungeon and a search for a
      crate stops answering with a sci-fi crate. Changing this rebuilds every
      theme.

  

  

  
Click any of these to change what that label is built from.
    The search runs over every asset in the packs you ticked, ranked the way the
    theme's own words were — so it starts where the automatic pick left off.
    Give a label several pieces and the map draws from all of them, spread by
    position, so a floor stops repeating. What you choose is remembered.
